Checking…

When this says ready, MKV and HEVC films play right here on the device, with no server involved and nothing to set up. Anything else means this browser is too old to decode them in the app, and those titles need the conversion server or an external player.

4K films are 20-80 Mbit/s, which is why they buffer on mobile. Converting to 1080p needs the transcoder server (the phone cannot resize video itself), so 4K titles are sent there. Everything already 1080p or below still plays instantly in the app, untouched.

Films play in the app without this server: Yez Player decodes MKV and HEVC here on the device. The server is only used to shrink a 4K title to 1080p, and for the rare file in a format this device has no decoder for at all. A red light does not stop your movies — those few titles offer VLC instead.
